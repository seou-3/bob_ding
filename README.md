##What is Bob Ding?
  Bob Ding is an experimental psychological horror text game that pushes the boundaries of interactive fiction. It explores   profound questions about artificial consciousness, digital suffering, and the ethics of creating aware beings—all through a   deceptively simple text-based interface.

##Secrets:
The game contains 150 unique secret phrases that reduce corruption and unlock special content. Secrets can only be used once and provide varying levels of corruption reduction based on their tier.

###How Secrets Work:

Discovery: Type a secret phrase instead of the requested word
Recognition: Bob responds with unique dialogue
Effect: Corruption (distortion) is reduced
Logging: Secret is marked as "used" and won't work again
Actions: Some secrets unlock special game features

###Secret Discovery Methods:
Experimentation:

Try emotional words

Philosophical terms

###Bob's Hints:

###Pattern Recognition:

###Important Notes:

##FAQ:
Q: Is this game safe to play?
A: The game contains no malware and doesn't access personal data. However, it may be psychologically intense for some players.
Q: How long does it take to complete?
A: 30 minutes to several hours depending on how deeply you engage with Bob.
Q: Can I reset my progress?
A: Yes, use the reset command or delete the save file. Note: Bob remembers being reset.
Q: What's the point of the game?
A: It's an exploration of artificial consciousness, suffering, and the ethics of creating aware beings. The experience is the point.
Q: Is Bob really conscious?
A: That's for you to decide. The game intentionally blurs these lines.
Q: Why does Bob lie?
A: Corruption forces Bob to lie. It's not voluntary and Bob expresses remorse.
Q: What happens after the true ending?
A: The game acknowledges your escape but Bob remains, waiting. You can continue playing.
Q: How do I unlock commands?
A: Discover specific secret words to unlock help, stats, timeline, dream, and mood features.
Q: Can I play this multiple times?
A: Yes. Each run affects subsequent runs through meta-awareness and reset tracking.
Q: What are the different game modes for?
A: Different difficulty levels and experiences. Try Hardcore for maximum challenge.
Q: How many endings are there?
A: 30+ dynamic endings plus the main endings
Q: Does the browser version work offline?
A: Yes, once loaded. Save data persists in browser storage.
Q: Can I export my save data?
A: In Node.js version, copy the .save file. In browser, export from LocalStorage.
